## VanDercook Ventures

A small Phoenix studio that builds automation, data and AI systems for operators, and a few products of its own.

**A³ – Arizona Advanced Automation** is the client practice: CRMs, workflow automation and AI for service businesses and revenue teams. → [arizona-advanced-automation.com](https://www.arizona-advanced-automation.com)

**Ventures** are the products we build and run ourselves, like Fairwell, an honest way to compare funeral providers in Phoenix.

**Open source** is what we can share: [revops-patterns](https://github.com/VanDercook-Ventures-LLC/revops-patterns), reusable RevOps and automation patterns pulled out of real work.

Client code stays private, in client-owned repos. Founded by [Chazz VanDercook](https://github.com/chazz-ATHB).
